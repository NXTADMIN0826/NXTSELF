The day itself

What to expect.

Before

Preparation

  • A consultation about your goals, medical history and medications
  • The skin is cleansed and disinfected
  • A topical numbing cream may be applied for comfort
During

Treatment

  • Tiny, superficial injections into the skin using a very fine needle
  • The dose is spread evenly across each area being treated
  • Performed under strict aseptic technique by a qualified practitioner
After

Aftercare

  • Small bumps and redness usually settle within hours to a day or two
  • No make-up for about 12 hours, and avoid touching the area
  • Avoid sauna, intense exercise and strong sun for 24–48 hours
  • Wear sunscreen daily throughout your course

Is KLIFT right for me? KLIFT is not used during pregnancy or breastfeeding, where there is active infection, cold sores or inflammation at the site, or if you are allergic to any component. Tell your practitioner about any autoimmune condition, immunosuppression or blood-thinning medication. Your practitioner will always follow the official Instructions for Use.

FAQ

Your questions, answered.

Can't find what you need? Ask a KLIFT practitioner, or contact us.

What is KLIFT?

KLIFT is an injectable skin booster made in Italy. One vial combines twelve active components: two weights of hyaluronic acid, collagen-building amino acids, antioxidants, a biomimetic peptide, DMAE and nucleic-acid fragments. Together they hydrate, rebuild, protect and firm the skin.

Is it a filler? Will it change my face?

No. KLIFT doesn't add volume or change the shape of your features. It improves the quality of the skin itself, including hydration, texture, tone and firmness, so you look fresh rather than different.

Can I combine KLIFT with other treatments?

Yes. Practitioners often use skin boosters alongside fillers and botulinum toxin. KLIFT improves skin quality, while fillers restore volume and toxin relaxes dynamic lines. It can also be applied topically straight after microneedling, fractional laser or RF microneedling. Your practitioner will plan the timing.

Does it hurt?

Most people find it very tolerable. A very fine needle is used, and a topical numbing cream can be applied beforehand.

When will I see results?

Hydration and glow often show early. Improvements in firmness and texture build over the course of 2–4 sessions as the skin renews itself. Results vary from person to person.

How long do results last?

To maintain results, the course is usually repeated after 6–9 months, or a single touch-up session can be scheduled monthly. Your practitioner will suggest the right rhythm for your skin.

Is there any downtime?

Downtime is minimal. Small bumps and redness at the injection points typically settle within hours to a day or two. Bruising is possible, especially if you take blood thinners.

Where can I get KLIFT?

KLIFT is supplied only to qualified medical practitioners. If you are a patient, ask your practitioner about KLIFT. In Kuwait, KLIFT is supplied exclusively by Rida Pharma (+965 6501 9421). Clinics can order through our order form.

Can KLIFT be used on the lips and under the eyes?

Yes, these are among KLIFT's target areas. On the lips, it hydrates and helps them look smoother and more defined, without the volume of a filler. Under the eyes, it helps the area look brighter, smoother and more rested. Your practitioner will decide the dose and technique for these delicate areas.
