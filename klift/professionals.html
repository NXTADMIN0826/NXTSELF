Protocol & dosing

Different areas, different needs.

The protocol follows the Glogau photo-ageing classification. Sessions are spaced every two weeks. Repeat the cycle after 6–9 months, or give a single monthly session as a touch-up.

Glogau typeWrinklesTypical ageCharacteristicsKLIFT course
Type I · MildNo wrinkles20s–30sEarly photo-ageing, mild pigment changes, no keratoses2 sessions · 1 mL per area
Type II · ModerateWrinkles in motionLate 30s–40sEarly lentigines, parallel smile lines beginning2 sessions · 1 mL per area
Type III · AdvancedWrinkles at rest50s+Obvious dyschromia, telangiectasia, visible keratoses3–4 sessions · 1.5 mL per area
Type IV · SevereOnly wrinkles60s–70sYellow-grey skin, wrinkles throughout3–4 sessions · 1.5 mL per area

Clinical practice

Technique & patient selection.

Below is general skin-booster good practice. Always follow the official KLIFT Instructions for Use for technique, contraindications, warnings and storage.

Technique

  • Cleanse and disinfect; topical anaesthetic if needed
  • Fine needle (e.g. 30–32G) or mesotherapy injector
  • Superficial intradermal micro-papules or nappage
  • Even distribution of 1 or 1.5 mL per area
  • Strict aseptic technique; single-patient use

Contraindications

  • Pregnancy and breastfeeding
  • Active infection, herpes or inflammation at the site
  • Known allergy to any component
  • Autoimmune disease or immunosuppression (clinical judgement)
  • Anticoagulants: higher bruising risk

Aftercare

  • Papules and redness settle in hours to a day or two
  • No make-up for ~12 h; avoid touching the area
  • Avoid sauna, intense exercise and strong sun for 24–48 h
  • Daily sunscreen throughout the course
  • Book the next session in 2 weeks
