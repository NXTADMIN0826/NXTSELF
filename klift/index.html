What is KLIFT

Not a filler. A skin-quality treatment that works from within.

KLIFT is an intradermal skin booster. It doesn't add volume or change your features. It delivers hyaluronic acid, collagen-building amino acids, antioxidants, a biomimetic peptide, DMAE and nucleic-acid fragments into the dermis, all in a single vial.

All treatment FAQs
Is KLIFT a filler?

No. KLIFT doesn't add volume or reshape your features. It is a skin booster that improves skin quality: hydration, texture, firmness and tone. Many practitioners combine it with fillers or toxin for a complete approach.

Who can have KLIFT?

KLIFT is designed for all degrees of skin ageing. Younger skin is treated for prevention and glow, and more mature skin for repair and firmness. Your practitioner will check whether it's suitable for you; for example, it is not used during pregnancy or breastfeeding.

How many sessions will I need?

Usually 2 sessions for younger skin and 3–4 sessions for more mature skin, spaced two weeks apart. To maintain results, the course can be repeated after 6–9 months, or you can have a single monthly touch-up.

Is there downtime?

Small bumps and some redness at the injection points usually settle within hours to a day or two. KLIFT contains mannitol, an antioxidant used in HA injectables that helps limit swelling after injection.
